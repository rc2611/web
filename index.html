About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate at the Indian Institute of Technology Delhi (IIT Delhi), specializing in Water Resources Engineering. My research fundamentally focuses on understanding the complex interactions between atmospheric processes and hydrological extremes.

I completed my B.Tech in Civil Engineering from the National Institute of Technology Hamirpur (NIT Hamirpur) and an M.S. by Research from the Indian Institute of Technology Palakkad (IIT Palakkad).

Currently, my work explores atmospheric moisture transport and its direct influence on extreme rainfall and flood dynamics. By employing a synthesis of data-driven methodologies and process-based models, I aim to enhance our predictive capabilities and understanding of changing climate paradigms.

Research Interests

Atmospheric Moisture Transport

Analyzing vapor flux patterns and their broad hydrological implications across varying topographies.

Hydrological Extremes

Investigating the mechanics underlying floods, droughts, and unprecedented precipitation variability.

Water Resources Management

Developing sustainable, adaptive approaches to manage water resources amidst global climate change.

Hydrological Modeling

Enhancing the predictive accuracy and efficiency of process-based models like SWAT.

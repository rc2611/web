About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Water Resources Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Delhi (IIT Delhi). My research investigates the complex mechanics of hydrological extremes, atmospheric moisture transport, and basin-scale flood dynamics under changing climate regimes.

Prior to IIT Delhi, I completed my M.S. by Research from IIT Palakkad (with a semester exchange at IIT Madras) and my B.Tech in Civil Engineering from NIT Hamirpur.

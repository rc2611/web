Biography & Overview

Dr. Rajat Choudhary is currently an Ox-CSC-AI Fellow at the University of Oxford, specializing in Water Resources Engineering, hydroclimatology, and AI-driven land-atmosphere interactions. His academic focus lies in uncovering how large-scale climate variability and atmospheric moisture transport dictate regional extreme precipitation regimes and catastrophic flood dynamics across global river basins.

He completed his Ph.D. in Water Resources Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Delhi (IIT Delhi), an M.S. by Research from IIT Palakkad (with coursework at IIT Madras), and his undergraduate degree in Civil Engineering from NIT Hamirpur.

Research Focus & Areas

Current investigations center around process-based simulation, atmospheric tracking, and climate adaptation frameworks:

  • Atmospheric Moisture Transport & Vapor Flux
  • Hydrological Extremes & Extreme Floods
  • SWAT & Distributed Hydrological Modeling
  • Land Surface Processes & Soil Moisture Feedback
  • Climate Change Impact Assessment
